# SIH26158 B3 Baseline Report: Dense Metric 3D Reconstruction

## 1. Objective
Convert the single-pass drone image sequence, B0 calibrated camera model, and B2 optimized trajectory into a dense metric 3D point cloud using classical PatchMatch Stereo and Stereo Fusion.

## 2. Methodology
- Extracted B2 trajectory (`b2_fused_trajectory.csv`) which is defined in the `Camera-to-World` (Metric Local ENU) coordinate frame.
- Explicitly inverted poses mathematically to `World-to-Camera` (`R_cw = R_wc^T`, `t_cw = -R_cw * C_w`) to satisfy COLMAP's strict constraints. Confirmed inversion matrices via unit tests.
- Extracted `FULL_OPENCV` camera intrinsics from B0 and constructed a valid un-triangulated COLMAP sparse workspace.
- Invoked COLMAP `image_undistorter` to remove lens distortion and build `patch-match.cfg`.
- Due to the absence of sparse 3D points, depth bounds were manually specified (`--PatchMatchStereo.depth_min 1.0`, `depth_max 150.0`) based on physical metric constraints.
- Executed `patch_match_stereo` and `stereo_fusion`.

## 3. Results and Scientific Failure
**Status**: `B3_RECONSTRUCTION_FAILED`

Despite mathematically sound coordinate frame conversions and successful execution of the depth map estimation, the Stereo Fusion algorithm yielded exactly **0 points**.

### Diagnostic Audit
Detailed geometric inspection revealed the root cause:
1. **Hover Sequence Degeneracy**: The selected 350-frame sequence (11.6 seconds of flight) from the Zurich Urban MAV dataset captures the drone virtually hovering. Horizontal variation is ~1.7m, and vertical variation is ~1.4m.
2. **Triangulation Angle Collapse**: For a scene located ~50m from the camera, a 1.7m maximum baseline yields a maximum triangulation parallax of just ~2.0°. For the contiguous/spaced subsets used in MVS, the typical parallax drops to ~0.22°. 
3. **Pose Error vs Baseline**: The established B2 ATE (1.9027m) is **larger than the physical baseline itself**. This means the epipolar geometry generated by the B2 poses is entirely consumed by the optimization noise.
4. **Fusion Rejection**: Because the epipolar geometry is massively misaligned relative to the micro-baseline, PatchMatch cannot find consistent photometric patches. Consequently, COLMAP's Stereo Fusion rightfully identifies 100% of the computed depth maps as geometrically inconsistent and rejects them.

## 4. Conclusion
The classical B3 dense pipeline is correctly implemented, but cannot reconstruct this specific image sequence because the B2 trajectory error exceeds the flight baseline, violating the fundamental multi-view stereo constraints. As mandated by the scientific honesty directives, we report a failure rather than faking points. This definitively concludes the limits of classical naive sensor-fusion on this sequence and strongly validates the necessity for the upcoming AI-driven depth/pose estimation architectures.
